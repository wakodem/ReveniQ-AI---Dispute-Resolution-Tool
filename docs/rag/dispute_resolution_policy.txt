## Rejection Fee Dispute

### Overview
A rejection fee (charge code = 'DCK') is raised on a customer account when a
Direct Debit (DD) payment is returned / rejected by the bank. The bank rejection
causes the original DD payment to be automatically backed out (ACTIVITY_TYPE = 'BCK').
A customer may dispute this fee if they had already made a cash payment before the
DD due date. The AI must validate whether the conditions for approval are met by
executing the SQL steps below in order.

### Key Terms and Codes
- PAYMENT_METHOD 'CA'  = Cash payment
- PAYMENT_METHOD 'DD'  = Direct Debit payment
- charge_code    'DCK' = Rejection fee charge
- ACTIVITY_TYPE  'BCK' = Payment backout / reversal event
- REVERSAL_REASON 'BCK'= Backout reason code on payment details
- STATUS         'O'   = Open dispute (pending resolution)

### Payment Types
- Payments can be posted as:
  - Cash Payment        → PAYMENT_METHOD = 'CA'
  - Direct Debit Payment → PAYMENT_METHOD = 'DD'

### Backout Logic
- Direct Debit payments are automatically backed out when rejected by the bank.
- Cash payments are usually backed out manually by a user.
- Rejection fee charge code = 'DCK'.
- A rejection fee backout is recorded in:
  - AR1_PAYMENT_DETAILS.REVERSAL_REASON = 'BCK'
  - AR1_PAYMENT_ACTIVITY.ACTIVITY_TYPE  = 'BCK'

### Approval Criteria (Policy)
A Rejection Fee Dispute is APPROVED ONLY IF ALL of the following are true:
  1. A BCK (backout) event exists for the account.
  2. A DCK (rejection fee) charge was created for the account.
  3. A Cash payment (PAYMENT_METHOD = 'CA') exists with a deposit_date
     BETWEEN AR1_DIRECT_DEBIT_REQUEST.EXTRACT_DATE
         AND AR1_DIRECT_DEBIT_REQUEST.DUE_DATE (inclusive).

REJECT if ANY of the following are true:
  - No BCK event found for the account.
  - No DCK charge created for the account.
  - No cash payment at all during the DD extract-to-due_date window.

### Edge Cases
- Boundary dates: BETWEEN is inclusive; a cash payment on exactly extract_date
  or exactly due_date qualifies for approval.
- No open dispute found in Step 1: Halt execution and flag for manual review.
  Do NOT proceed with subsequent steps.
- Multiple DCK charges: Any DCK charge linked to the correct pay_channel_no
  and effective_date satisfies the condition.
- Null extract_date or due_date in direct debit request: Flag for manual review;
  cannot determine the qualifying window.

### Manual Review Triggers — “Funds Were Available” / Bank Statement Claims (Rejection Fee)
Use this subsection when the dispute memo (or customer narrative) indicates the customer
believes their bank account had enough money to cover the debit, or that Telkom failed
to debit despite available funds. These cases are NOT decidable from the standard
“cash payment between extract_date and due_date” rule alone. The bank’s return reason,
timing, weekend/public-holiday alignment, duplicate or missed collection attempts,
and third‑party statement evidence must be checked by a human or specialist process.

#### When to route to Manual Review (mandatory)
Classify as **Manual Review** when ANY of the following intent signals appear in the memo
or attachment notes (paraphrases and spelling variants count):

- Customer says **money was in the account**, **funds were available**, **sufficient funds**,
  **enough balance**, **had funds when the debit went off**, or **balance covered the debit**.
- Customer says the **debit order returned / bounced / rejected** even though **funds were there**.
- Customer provides or references a **bank statement** (attached on CRM, email, mobile CRM)
  to prove **available balance** on the debit date or around the rejection event.
- Customer claims **Telkom did not debit** / **no debit attempt** / **wrong debit date**
  while asserting **funds were available** in the stated period.
- Customer claims **funds were available for months** but **no successful debit**,
  sometimes with **penalty rejection fees** stacking.
- Customer disputes **insufficient funds** as the reason, saying the bank statement
  shows **funds were available** (contradiction between bank narrative and billing system).

#### Phrase clusters for retrieval (embedding-friendly synonyms)
Group A — balance / availability:
  “money in the account”, “funds in the account”, “funds available”, “sufficient funds”,
  “enough funds”, “enough money”, “had balance”, “remaining balance in the bank account”,
  “client had funds”, “money was there for the debit”.

Group B — event mismatch:
  “debit order went off but returned”, “debit rejected although money available”,
  “DD bounced but funds there”, “transaction failed to deduct”, “Telkom did not debit”.

Group C — evidence:
  “bank statement attached”, “statement on CRM”, “proof attached”, “see attached statement”,
  “circled on statement”, “POP attached”.

#### Representative memo patterns (training / RAG examples)
The following are stylistic templates seen in live disputes; they all imply **Manual Review**:

1. Refund or credit requested because **rejection fee was charged but there was money in the account**.
2. **Client had funds when the debit order ran** but the payment was **returned for an unknown reason**;
   customer is distressed.
3. **Bank statement attached**; customer **had enough funds on [date]** but was still charged
   **payment rejection fee**; asks to dispute or reverse the fee.
4. **Sufficient funds in the customer’s personal bank account**; **bank statement on mobile CRM**.
5. Credit the **rejection fee**; **funds were available** in **January and December** but
   **Telkom did not debit**; statement under attachments.
6. Charged **R202.70 rejection fee** but **funds were available for Telkom to deduct**.
7. **Debit rejected** in [month] **yet customer had money in the account**; review **bank statement on the system**.
8. Customer **had enough funds**; **bank statement on CRM** for the debit / penalty period.
9. **Funds available on the account**; request to **waive or credit** rejection **penalty R202.70**;
   **bank statement** supplied.
10. Dispute **rejection fee** because **money was available**; **attached statement**.
11. Account / statement shows **enough money for the debit order to succeed**; **statement on CRM**.
12. **Balance in account** (e.g. specific amount) when debit failed; **refer to bank statement**.
13. Bank shows **rejection reason insufficient funds** but customer says **statement proves funds available**
    — requires reconciliation.
14. **Always enough funds** for the debit; **bank statement attached** to support the claim.
15. **Not debited for multiple months** though **money was available to deduct**; **penalty rejection fee** charged.

#### Agent and AI guidance
- Do **not** treat these memos as automatic **APPROVE** or **REJECT** using only the standard
  SQL path in this document. Mark **Manual Review** and record the trigger phrases.
- Required evidence for resolution typically includes: **bank statement** for the relevant dates,
  **DD extract/due dates**, **return reason codes** from collections / bank, and whether
  **Telkom attempted debit** on the correct date and amount.
- If the only approved path in policy is a **cash (CA) payment in the DD window**, but the
  customer’s story is strictly **“funds sat in the bank account”** without a qualifying CA
  payment, that is a **process mismatch** — still **Manual Review**, not a blind REJECT
  without checking return codes and debit attempts.

### SQL Validation Steps
-- NOTE: All SQL uses -- for inline comments (ANSI standard).
-- Execute steps in order. Each step documents where its input values come from.
-- Replace ? placeholders with actual values obtained from the previous step.

-- STEP 1: Get account_id for the open dispute.
-- Input  : dispute_id supplied by the application.
-- Output : account_id → used in Steps 2, 3, 5a, 5b, 5c, 5d.
SELECT *
FROM   ar1_dispute
WHERE  STATUS     = 'O'
AND    dispute_id = ?;          -- substitute actual dispute_id

-- STEP 2: Find the most recent payment backout (BCK) on this account.
-- Input  : account_id from Step 1.
-- Output : credit_id, last_bck_payment_date → used in Steps 5a and 4 (as effective_date).
-- If no rows returned → REJECT (no BCK event exists).
SELECT account_id,
       credit_id,
       activity_date AS last_bck_payment_date
FROM (
    SELECT account_id,
           credit_id,
           activity_date,
           ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
               PARTITION BY account_id
               ORDER BY activity_date DESC
           ) AS rn
    FROM   ar1_payment_activity
    WHERE  account_id    = ?    -- account_id from Step 1
    AND    activity_type = 'BCK'
) bck
WHERE bck.rn = 1;

-- STEP 3: Get the pay_channel_id for this account.
-- Input  : account_id from Step 1.
-- Output : PAY_CHANNEL_ID → used in Step 4 as pay_channel_no.
SELECT PAY_CHANNEL_ID
FROM   ar1_pay_channel
WHERE  account_id = ?;          -- account_id from Step 1

-- STEP 4: Confirm that a DCK (rejection fee) charge was created.
-- Input  : PAY_CHANNEL_ID from Step 3 (used as pay_channel_no).
--          last_bck_payment_date  from Step 2 (used as EFFECTIVE_DATE).
-- Output : Existence of a DCK charge row.
-- If no rows returned → REJECT (no rejection fee was raised; nothing to dispute).
SELECT *
FROM   bl1_charge_request
WHERE  pay_channel_no  = ?      -- PAY_CHANNEL_ID from Step 3
AND    charge_code     = 'DCK'
AND    (effective_date = ? OR effective_date = ? + 1);  -- both ? values = last_bck_payment_date

-- STEP 5: Check whether a qualifying cash payment exists.
-- This step has four sub-queries executed in sequence.

-- STEP 5a: Get payment record(s) for this account linked to the BCK credit.
-- Input  : account_id from Step 1, credit_id from Step 2.
-- Output : payment_id → used in Step 5b.
SELECT *
FROM   ar1_payment
WHERE  account_id = ?           -- account_id from Step 1
AND    credit_id  = ?;          -- credit_id from Step 2

-- STEP 5b: Get payment detail with reversal_reason = 'BCK' to find the
--          Direct Debit voucher number.
-- Input  : account_id from Step 1, payment_id from Step 5a.
-- Output : DIRECT_DEBIT_VOUCHER → used in Step 5c.
SELECT *
FROM   ar1_payment_details
WHERE  account_id      = ?      -- account_id from Step 1
AND    payment_id      = ?      -- payment_id from Step 5a
AND    reversal_reason = 'BCK'; -- confirms this is the DD backout record

-- STEP 5c: Retrieve the rejected Direct Debit request to get the
--          qualifying date window.
-- Input  : account_id from Step 1, DIRECT_DEBIT_VOUCHER from Step 5b.
-- Output : EXTRACT_DATE, DUE_DATE → used in Step 5d.
-- If no rows returned → Flag for manual review (missing DD request record).
SELECT *
FROM   ar1_direct_debit_request
WHERE  account_id           = ?  -- account_id from Step 1
AND    DIRECT_DEBIT_VOUCHER = ?; -- DIRECT_DEBIT_VOUCHER from Step 5b

-- STEP 5d: Check if a cash payment (CA) was made within the qualifying window.
-- Input  : account_id from Step 1.
--          EXTRACT_DATE from Step 5c.
--          DUE_DATE     from Step 5c.
-- Output : Existence of a cash payment row in the qualifying window.
-- If rows returned → APPROVE.
-- If no rows returned → REJECT.
SELECT *
FROM   ar1_payment_details
WHERE  account_id      = ?           -- account_id from Step 1
AND    deposit_date    BETWEEN ?     -- EXTRACT_DATE from Step 5c
                           AND ?     -- DUE_DATE from Step 5c (inclusive)
AND    payment_method  = 'CA';

### Decision Rules Summary
After executing all SQL steps, apply this decision matrix:

| Condition                                              | Decision        |
|--------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Memo matches “funds available / bank statement” triggers (see subsection above) | Manual Review first |
| Step 1 returns no open dispute                         | Manual Review   |
| Step 2 returns no BCK event                            | REJECT          |
| Step 4 returns no DCK charge                           | REJECT          |
| Step 5c returns no DD request record                   | Manual Review   |
| Step 5d returns cash payment within extract–due window | APPROVE         |
| Step 5d returns no qualifying cash payment             | REJECT          |
| extract_date or due_date is null                       | Manual Review   |

### Audit / Notes for Agent
- Always record which SQL steps were executed and their outcomes in the
  dispute audit log before submitting an Approve / Reject decision.
- For Manual Review cases, escalate to the senior back-office team with
  the step number and reason for escalation.


## Late Payment / Interest Dispute

### Overview
A late payment fee (charge code = 'LATE_PYM') is raised when a customer pays their
bill after the billing due date. However, there are cases where the payment was
received on time but posted in the system after the due date — in such cases, the
charge should be credited. The deposit_date in ar1_payment_details reflects the
actual payment date, while SYS_CREATION_DATE reflects when the payment was recorded
in the system.

### Key Terms and Codes
- charge_code     'LATE_PYM' = Late payment fee charge
- BALANCE         = Outstanding balance on the charge group
- RESTRICTION_AMOUNT         = Amount already credited against the charge
- DEPOSIT_DATE               = Actual date payment was received
- SYS_CREATION_DATE          = System posting date of the payment
- DUE_DATE                   = Billing due date from the charge group

### Approval Criteria (Policy)
A Late Payment / Interest Dispute is APPROVED ONLY IF ALL of the following are true:
  1. A LATE_PYM charge exists for the account linked to the dispute.
  2. The account balance (BALANCE) on the charge group is zero (fully paid).
  3. No prior credit (RESTRICTION_AMOUNT = 0) has been applied to the charge.
  4. A payment exists where PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE (SYS_CREATION_DATE) is on or
     before the DUE_DATE — meaning the payment was received on time but possibly
     posted late in the system.

REJECT if ANY of the following are true:
  - Outstanding balance (BALANCE > 0) remains unpaid as of the due date.
  - A credit (RESTRICTION_AMOUNT > 0) has already been applied to the charge.
  - The payment was received (PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE) after the due date.

### Manual Review Triggers
Route to MANUAL REVIEW when:
  - No matching LATE_PYM charge is found for the dispute (reason code: NO_CHARGE_FOUND).
  - No payment record is found after the due date for the account (reason code: NO_PAYMENT_FOUND).
  - Customer claims system error, system delay, or bank processing delay caused the
    late posting — requires investigation into system logs or bank confirmation.

### Edge Cases
- Boundary dates: A payment with PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE exactly equal to DUE_DATE
  qualifies for approval (on or before).
- Multiple LATE_PYM charges: The most recent charge before the dispute creation date
  is used (ROW_NUMBER with ORDER BY CREATION_DATE DESC, rn = 1).
- Multiple charge groups: The most recent charge group with DUE_DATE before the
  charge creation date is used (ROW_NUMBER with ORDER BY DUE_DATE DESC, rn2 = 1).

### SQL Validation Steps
-- NOTE: All SQL uses -- for inline comments (ANSI standard).
-- Execute steps in order. Each step documents where its input values come from.
-- Replace ? placeholders with actual values obtained from the previous step.

-- STEP 1: Validate the charge and check for existing credits or outstanding balance.
-- Input  : dispute_id supplied by the application.
-- Output : ACCOUNT_ID, CHARGE_ID, BALANCE, DUE_DATE, RESTRICTION_AMOUNT,
--          CHARGE_CREATION_DATE → used in Step 2.
-- Rules  :
--   IF no rows returned → MANUAL REVIEW (reason code: NO_CHARGE_FOUND).
--   IF BALANCE > 0 → REJECT (outstanding balance remains).
--   IF RESTRICTION_AMOUNT > 0 → REJECT (credit already applied).
--   IF BALANCE = 0 AND RESTRICTION_AMOUNT = 0 → proceed to Step 2.
SELECT DISPUTE_ID, CHARGE_ID, ACCOUNT_ID, BILLING_ARRANGEMENT_ID, INVOICE_ID,
       AMOUNT, CHARGE_CODE, CHARGE_CREATION_DATE, DISPUTE_CREATION_DATE,
       DEBIT_ID, CHARGES_AMOUNT, TAX_AMOUNT, BALANCE, DUE_DATE, RESTRICTION_AMOUNT
FROM (
    SELECT base.DISPUTE_ID, base.CHARGE_ID, base.ACCOUNT_ID, base.BILLING_ARRANGEMENT_ID,
           base.INVOICE_ID, base.AMOUNT, base.CHARGE_CODE, base.CHARGE_CREATION_DATE,
           base.DISPUTE_CREATION_DATE, base.RESTRICTION_AMOUNT,
           cg.DEBIT_ID, cg.CHARGES_AMOUNT, cg.TAX_AMOUNT, cg.BALANCE, cg.DUE_DATE,
           ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY base.DISPUTE_ID ORDER BY cg.DUE_DATE DESC) AS rn2
    FROM (
        SELECT DISPUTE_ID, CHARGE_ID, ACCOUNT_ID, BILLING_ARRANGEMENT_ID, INVOICE_ID,
               AMOUNT, CHARGE_CODE, CREATION_DATE AS CHARGE_CREATION_DATE, DISPUTE_CREATION_DATE,
               RESTRICTION_AMOUNT
        FROM (
            SELECT d.DISPUTE_ID,
                   c.CHARGE_ID, c.ACCOUNT_ID, c.BILLING_ARRANGEMENT_ID, c.INVOICE_ID,
                   c.AMOUNT, c.CHARGE_CODE, c.CREATION_DATE, c.RESTRICTION_AMOUNT,
                   d.SYS_CREATION_DATE AS DISPUTE_CREATION_DATE,
                   ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY d.DISPUTE_ID ORDER BY c.CREATION_DATE DESC) AS rn
            FROM ar1_dispute d
            JOIN ar1_charges c
              ON c.ACCOUNT_ID = d.ACCOUNT_ID
             AND c.CHARGE_CODE = 'LATE_PYM'
             AND c.CREATION_DATE < d.SYS_CREATION_DATE
            WHERE d.DISPUTE_ID IN (?)          -- substitute actual dispute_id
        )
        WHERE rn = 1
    ) base
    JOIN ar1_charge_group cg
      ON cg.ACCOUNT_ID = base.ACCOUNT_ID
     AND cg.BILLING_ARRANGEMENT_ID = base.BILLING_ARRANGEMENT_ID
     AND cg.DUE_DATE < base.CHARGE_CREATION_DATE
)
WHERE rn2 = 1
ORDER BY DISPUTE_ID;

-- STEP 2: Verify the actual payment date against the due date.
-- Input  : ACCOUNT_ID from Step 1, DUE_DATE from Step 1.
-- Output : PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE, PAYMENT_AMOUNT.
-- Rules  :
--   IF no rows returned → MANUAL REVIEW (reason code: NO_PAYMENT_FOUND).
--   IF PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE <= DUE_DATE → APPROVE (payment received on time).
--   IF PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE > DUE_DATE → REJECT (payment received late).
SELECT PAYMENT_ID, ACCOUNT_ID, DEPOSIT_DATE, REVERSAL_REASON,
       SYS_CREATION_DATE AS PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE, AMOUNT AS PAYMENT_AMOUNT
FROM (
    SELECT p.PAYMENT_ID, p.ACCOUNT_ID, p.DEPOSIT_DATE, p.REVERSAL_REASON,
           p.SYS_CREATION_DATE, p.AMOUNT,
           ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY p.SYS_CREATION_DATE ASC) AS rn
    FROM ar1_payment_details p
    WHERE p.ACCOUNT_ID = ?                    -- ACCOUNT_ID from Step 1
      AND p.SYS_CREATION_DATE > ?            -- DUE_DATE from Step 1
)
WHERE rn = 1;

### Decision Rules Summary
After executing all SQL steps, apply this decision matrix:

| Condition                                                          | Decision        | Reason Code       |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Step 1 returns no rows (no LATE_PYM charge found)                  | Manual Review   | NO_CHARGE_FOUND   |
| Step 1 BALANCE > 0                                                 | REJECT          | —                 |
| Step 1 RESTRICTION_AMOUNT > 0                                      | REJECT          | —                 |
| Step 2 returns no rows (no payment after due date)                 | Manual Review   | NO_PAYMENT_FOUND  |
| Step 2 PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE <= DUE_DATE                           | APPROVE         | —                 |
| Step 2 PAYMENT_CREATION_DATE > DUE_DATE                            | REJECT          | —                 |
| Memo indicates system error / delay / bank processing issue        | Manual Review   | —                 |

### Audit / Notes for Agent
- Always record which SQL steps were executed and their outcomes in the
  dispute audit log before submitting an Approve / Reject decision.
- For Manual Review cases, include the reason code (NO_CHARGE_FOUND or
  NO_PAYMENT_FOUND) to help CSRs triage faster.
- For APPROVE, specify: "Initiate charge-level credit on CHARGE_ID {x}."
- For REJECT, specify: "No action — charge stands."


## Subscription / FA (Administrative)

### Overview
This category covers administrative disputes where subscription charges and/or FA
(Financial Account) mapping are questioned, but the memo often does not provide
enough structured detail to decide purely from text. Typical themes include:
- "Subscription - FA"
- "Resolution Protection - FA"
- "POA ongoing / pending"
- customer requests to move products to a single FA
- subscription billed with no usage or migration/admin mismatch

This category is operationally different from pure rejection-fee logic:
it is primarily an account structure + billing alignment investigation.

### Typical Patterns Seen In Memo Data (118 samples)
- Short memos with minimal context (often only "Subscription - FA").
- "Resolution Protection" references that imply interim handling / pending actions.
- Product/line should bill on one FA, but charges appear on another FA.
- Customer claims no usage yet subscription billed.
- POA pending/ongoing notes where final decision is deferred.

### High-Level Decision Policy
Use the following outcome framework:

1) APPROVE (credit/adjust) when:
   - subscription charge is posted to the wrong FA and customer impact is confirmed, OR
   - duplicate/misconfigured subscription charge is verified, OR
   - service migration/admin handling created unintended subscription billing.

2) REJECT when:
   - charge is valid, mapped to the correct FA, and usage/subscription entitlement exists, OR
   - no evidence of billing/admin error is found in AR/Invoicing records.

3) MANUAL REVIEW when:
   - memo lacks required identifiers and records are ambiguous,
   - POA/protection workflow is still in progress,
   - cross-system mapping (AR vs BL) is inconsistent and needs senior validation.

### Suggested Data Model Anchors (AR + Invoicing)
Use these entities as evidence points:

Accounts Receivable side:
- AR1_DISPUTE (dispute header/status/account linkage)
- AR1_ACCOUNT (FA/account identity)
- AR1_PAY_CHANNEL (pay channel to account/FA mapping)
- AR1_INVOICE (invoice records by account)
- AR1_CHARGES (posted charges)
- AR1_MEMO (memo/audit context)

Invoicing side:
- BL1_PAY_CHANNEL (billing pay-channel linkage)
- BL1_INVOICE (invoice header and cycle context)
- BL1_CHARGE / BL1_CHARGE_REQUEST (charge-level generation records)
- BL1_INV_CHARGE_REL (invoice-to-charge linkage)
- BL1_CUSTOMER / BL1_CUSTOMER_CHARGES (customer-level charge view)

### Agent Notes (How AI should answer)
- Prioritize factual wording and evidence references.
- If evidence is weak, choose Manual Review instead of forced Approve/Reject.
- Mention FA/account mapping findings explicitly in the resolution summary.
- For customer communication, keep recommended action concise and actionable.
